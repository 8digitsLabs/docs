The same stock can be a dozen different tokens. xStocks mints one on Solana, Ondo another on Ethereum, Coinbase and Robinhood mint their own on their own chains. They trade in separate pools, so at any moment they carry different prices, different depth and different spreads.

Buying the wrong one costs you money before the stock has moved.

oka prices all of them and fills the cheapest, measured all-in: the bridge to reach it, the spread you cross, and the gas it burns.

## What you get

**Over 700 stocks and ETFs**, across **five issuers** and **six chains**, close to three thousand listings, priced against each other every time you place an order.

**Everything in one place.** oka holds your wrappers as one portfolio, one line per company, with what you paid and what it is worth now. Open a line to see the issuers and chains behind it.

**One balance.** Deposits from any chain become USDC on Base. That one number is the cash you buy with.

**No seed phrase, no extension.** Sign in with an email address, Google, X or a passkey. Your wallets are created in the same moment, and you can export the keys whenever you want.

## What you are actually buying

Not a share. When you buy AAPL on oka you receive a **wrapper**: a token an issuer mints to track Apple's price. You get economic exposure to that price. You do not get legal title, a vote, or an entry on any shareholder register, and your claim is on the issuer rather than on Apple.

Which issuer's paper you hold is a real choice. Expert mode lets you make it; Auto makes it on cost.

## Why the cheapest listing is not obvious

A listing's price tells you little about what buying it will cost. A cent of spread advantage does not survive a three-dollar bridge, and depth starts to matter as soon as your order is large enough to move the pool.

oka simulates the whole path for every open listing and ranks them on the total.

## OKA and the index

**IOKA** is an index of five tokenized stocks, in one token. **OKA** has a key role in the governance of that fund, and you buy and sell it for IOKA. When the five stocks rise, OKA is priced in a token that rose with them.
