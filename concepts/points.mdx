Points come from using the app. They decide how the community incentive is split. Buying or selling OKA or IOKA earns nothing.

Four things count, and all four are about stock trades in the app:

- **Volume traded.** The stocks and ETFs you buy and sell.
- **Fees paid.** The platform fee on those trades.
- **People you refer** to the app.
- **Volume those people trade** in the app.

The last two sit on top of the 40% fee share. That share is paid separately, in stables, on the same stock trades.
