Hold stables across several chains and you end up with forty dollars on Solana, twelve on Arbitrum and a hundred and ten on Base, none of which can buy what the others can. oka collapses that into one number.

## The sweep

Deposit on any supported chain and your stables are bridged into **USDC on Base**, automatically, as their own step, before and independent of any order you place. It costs **\$1**, flat, which pays for the bridge. Deposit on Base and there is nothing to bridge, so it is free.

## What it buys

**One balance.** Your cash is a single figure. Nothing is stranded on a chain you have forgotten about, and nothing has to be moved by hand before you can use it.

**Cheap execution.** Base gas is cents. Every order starts there, so the fixed cost of touching a chain is the smallest it can be.

**A router with one job.** Every quote is priced the same way: USDC on Base, to the listing, wherever it lives. Comparing listings becomes comparing routes from a known starting point.

**Fewer failure modes.** A route that fails mid-flight comes home to Base. There is one place for money to be when something goes wrong, and one place to retry from.

## What it costs

**A dollar per deposit**, which matters most on small ones. A \$10 deposit pays 10%; a \$500 deposit pays 0.2%. Depositing more, less often, is cheaper, and depositing on Base is free.

**A bridge on the way out.** Selling a wrapper that lives on Solana brings the proceeds home to Base, and withdrawing to Solana bridges them back out. A round trip that starts and ends elsewhere crosses the bridge twice.

**A dependency.** Base being unavailable is oka being unavailable. That is the trade for everything above.
