---
title: "One balance"
description: "Your cash is a single figure in USDC on Base, whatever chain you sent it from."
---

Hold stables across several chains and you end up with forty dollars on Solana, twelve on Arbitrum and a hundred and ten on Base, none of which can buy what the others can. oka keeps your cash as one number instead.

That number is **USDC on Base**. Deposit from any supported chain and your stables are bridged there automatically, as their own step, before any order. It costs **\$1** flat, which pays for the bridge, and depositing on Base is free.

Every order starts from there. Base gas is cents, so the fixed cost of beginning a trade stays small, and every listing is quoted the same way: from USDC on Base, out to wherever the listing lives. A route that fails on the way comes home to Base, so there is one place for your money to be when something goes wrong.

## Coming back

Selling a wrapper that lives on Solana brings the proceeds home to Base. Withdrawing them to Solana bridges them out again, so a round trip that starts and ends off Base crosses the bridge twice.

## The one exception

Perps do not draw on this. They are margined from a **perps balance**, which sits on Lighter rather than in your execution wallet, and moving between the two is a transfer of a few minutes rather than a book entry.

It is the only balance on oka that is not your cash, and it exists because the money really is somewhere else. Everything above still holds for everything except perps.
