Most onchain products hand you a balance per chain and call it a feature. You end up with forty dollars on Solana, twelve on Arbitrum and a hundred and ten on Base, none of which can buy anything the others can.

oka collapses that into one number.

## The sweep

Deposit on any supported chain and your stables are bridged into **USDC on Base**, automatically, as their own step — before and independent of any order you place. It costs **$1**, flat. Deposit on Base and there is nothing to bridge, so it is free.

The dollar is the bridge's price, not a margin. What it buys is everything downstream.

## What it buys

**One balance.** Your cash is a single figure. Nothing is stranded on a chain you have forgotten about, and nothing has to be manually moved before you can use it.

**Cheap execution.** Base gas is cents. Every order starts there, so the fixed cost of touching a chain is the smallest it can be.

**A router with one job.** Quotes are all priced the same way — USDC on Base, to the listing, wherever it lives. The comparison between listings is a comparison of routes from a known starting point, not a puzzle about where your money currently sits.

**Fewer failure modes.** A route that fails mid-flight comes home to Base. There is one place for money to be when something goes wrong, and one place to retry from.

## What it costs

**A dollar per deposit**, which matters most on small ones. A $10 deposit pays 10%; a $500 deposit pays 0.2%. Depositing more, less often, is strictly cheaper — and depositing on Base costs nothing at all.

**A bridge on the way out.** Selling a wrapper that lives on Solana brings the proceeds home to Base, and withdrawing to Solana bridges them back out. A round trip that starts and ends elsewhere crosses the bridge twice.

**A dependency.** Base being unavailable is oka being unavailable. That is the trade for everything above, and it is worth naming rather than burying.

<Note>
  Deposits are detected on a check that runs when you open the portfolio and while the deposit dialog is open. A deposit below the chain's minimum is not picked up at all — it sits where you sent it until you top it up past the line.
</Note>
