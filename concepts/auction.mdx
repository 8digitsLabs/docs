## What you bid

**How much you will spend.** That USDG is locked until the sale settles.

**The most you will pay for one token.** The number of tokens follows from those two. Spend 1,000 at 2.00 and the bid is for 500 tokens.

The price starts at **0.10 USDG** and moves in steps of **0.10**. At 0.10, OKA is valued at a **100,000** market cap. A price you type is snapped down to the nearest step.

One bid per address, and it cannot ask for more than **1% of the supply**. Until the close you can add USDG or raise the price. You cannot lower it, spend less, or cancel.

## One price

Tokens go to the highest bids until the supply runs out. The price of the last token sold is the **clearing price**. Every filled bid pays it.

| Your bid | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Above** the clearing price | Filled in full. You pay the clearing price. The rest of the deposit comes back. |
| **Exactly** the clearing price | A share of what is left. You may get only part of what you asked for. |
| **Below** the clearing price | Nothing. The whole deposit comes back. |

If demand falls short of the supply, the clearing price is **0.10 USDG**. Bids at or above it are filled in full, and the unsold tokens stay unsold.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A clearing, in numbers">
    One million tokens on sale. Four bids:

    | Bidder | Tokens wanted | Max price | Deposit |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Alice | 300,000 | 3.00 | 900,000 |
    | Bob | 500,000 | 2.00 | 1,000,000 |
    | Carol | 400,000 | 1.50 | 600,000 |
    | Dave | 600,000 | 1.00 | 600,000 |

    At 3.00, demand is 300,000. At 2.00 it is 800,000. At 1.50 it reaches 1,200,000, which covers the million on sale. **1.50 is the clearing price.** At that step, 200,000 tokens are left for 400,000 demanded, so Carol is filled halfway.

    | Bidder | Tokens | Pays | Refund |
    |---|---|---|---|
    | Alice | 300,000 | 450,000 | 450,000 |
    | Bob | 500,000 | 750,000 | 250,000 |
    | Carol | 200,000 | 300,000 | 300,000 |
    | Dave | 0 | 0 | 600,000 |

    Alice bid 3.00 and pays 1.50. Dave is out, because the supply was gone before his price. Drop Carol and Dave and demand is only 800,000: the clearing price falls to the minimum, Alice and Bob are filled in full, and 200,000 tokens are unsold.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What stays hidden">
    What goes on chain is a commitment, a hash, plus the deposit. The price and the size travel encrypted. Keep the receipt the app saves on your device before the transaction is sent. Once the bid is opened, that receipt is how you check that what was revealed is what you sent.

    You do not open your own bid. oka does, after bidding closes. Other bidders cannot read your price or your size while the sale is open. oka can. A bid that is never opened is left out of the price, and the deposit comes back in full.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
