---
title: "Glossary"
description: "The words oka uses, and what each one means precisely."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Underlying">
    The real security, AAPL, the share Apple issues. It trades on a stock exchange during that exchange's hours. You never hold it on oka.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Wrapper">
    A token an issuer mints to track an underlying. AAPLx by xStocks tracks AAPL. Your claim is on the issuer, not on Apple. Two wrappers on the same stock are different instruments.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Listing">
    One wrapper deployed on one chain, AAPLx on Solana. Each listing has its own pool, depth and price. This is the unit oka actually trades, and what Auto mode ranks.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Exposure">
    What you hold after a trade settles: a wrapper balance whose value follows the underlying's price. Not legal title, no vote, no entry on any shareholder register.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Cash">
    USDC on Base, unspent. Deposits are swept here whichever chain they arrived from, sale proceeds come home here, and a failed order leaves your money here. Always spent before oka moves anything new in.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Execution wallet">
    The two wallets created at sign-in, one EVM, one Solana, presented as one. Where cash sits and wrappers land. Either can be exported from the account menu.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Session key">
    What lets oka execute the steps of a trade, sweeping a deposit to Base, filling, exiting, bringing a failed route home, without prompting at each one. Attached automatically at sign-in, with no prompt and no revoke button. It does not cover withdrawals.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="All-in price">
    Everything you pay between the cash you hold and the position you end up with: spread and price impact, the bridge when the route needs one, and the gas take. There is no oka fee in it today.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Fill">
    A buy. Cash in, wrapper out. All-or-nothing: it settles within 100 basis points of the quoted output or it does not happen.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Exit">
    A sale. Wrapper out, proceeds bridged home to Base as cash. Large exits can be split across several listings, and those legs settle independently.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Platform fee">
    Planned, not charged. A 0.25% fee on a trade, of which 40% goes to whoever referred you. Nothing of the kind appears on a ticket today.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Gas take">
    What oka charges in stables to cover the native-token gas it paid on your behalf. Estimated with a 20% buffer, floored at \$0.01, flat \$0.005 on Solana, and zero on a venue whose solver pays the gas. Scales with the chain, not with your order size.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Auto mode">
    The default. oka compares every listing that can fill and takes the one leaving you the most stock. You do not pick the issuer or the chain.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Expert mode">
    You choose the issuer and the chain from a table of every listing and its all-in cost. The execution venue stays out of your hands in both modes.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Settlement token">
    The stablecoin a chain settles in. USDC everywhere, except Robinhood Chain, which uses USDG: the Global Dollar.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
