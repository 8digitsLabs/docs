The order ticket names each of these before you confirm.

## Buying a stock

| | What it is | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| **Deposit** | \$1 flat, and free on Base | the bridge |
| **Spread** | what the pool gives you against its mid price, your size included | the market |
| **Gas** | charged in stables, so you never hold ETH, SOL or BNB | the chain |
| **Bridge** | charged when a route leaves Base to reach a listing, and again on the way home from a sale | the bridge |
| **Platform fee** | 0.25% on a trade, of which 40% goes to whoever referred you | oka |

The spread is the largest of these on most trades, and it is what routing exists to attack: comparing listings is mostly comparing spreads and depth.

Gas scales with the chain rather than with your order. That is what makes small trades on Ethereum unattractive, and why its minimums are higher.

<Note>
  Above **\$10,000**, a deposit can carry up to an extra **0.1%**, shown before you send.
</Note>

## Perps

A perp is priced differently, because what you pay is measured against the position rather than against what you spend.

| | What it is | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| **Trading fee** | 0.04% of the position's size, maker and taker alike, on the way in and again on the way out | oka |
| **Lighter fee** | 0.005% of the position's size, maker and taker, each way | Lighter |
| **Funding** | exchanged every 8 hours between longs and shorts, either direction | the other side of the trade |
| **Spread** | what the book gives you against its mid price | the market |

<Warning>
  The trading fee is a share of the **position**, not of your margin. Maker and taker pay the same 0.04%. At 20x, that is 0.8% of the margin you posted on the way in, and 1.6% across a round trip. Leverage multiplies the fee exactly as it multiplies everything else.
</Warning>

Opening a perps balance moves your account onto Lighter's paid tier, which is where the 0.005% comes from. The free tier charges nothing to trade but cannot carry oka's fee at all, so the two go together. You get lower latency and higher limits with it.

Liquidation is not a fee. Nothing is charged for it. It is the point at which your margin no longer covers the loss, and it costs you the margin itself.

## What is free

Holding a position. Having an account. Exporting your keys. Withdrawing costs you the chain's gas and nothing to oka. Moving money between your cash and your perps balance is free, though it is not instant.
