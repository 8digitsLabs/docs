The order ticket names each of these before you confirm.

| | What it is | Who gets it |
|---|---|---|
| **Deposit** | \$1 flat, and free on Base | the bridge |
| **Spread** | what the pool gives you against its mid price, your size included | the market |
| **Gas** | charged in stables, so you never hold ETH, SOL or BNB | the chain |
| **Bridge** | charged when a route leaves Base to reach a listing, and again on the way home from a sale | the bridge |
| **Platform fee** | 0.25% on a trade, of which 40% goes to whoever referred you | oka |

The spread is the largest of these on most trades, and it is what routing exists to attack: comparing listings is mostly comparing spreads and depth.

Gas scales with the chain rather than with your order. That is what makes small trades on Ethereum unattractive, and why its minimums are higher.

<Note>
  No platform fee is charged today. And above **\$10,000**, a deposit can carry up to an extra **0.1%**, shown before you send.
</Note>

## What is free

Holding a position. Having an account. Exporting your keys. Withdrawing costs you the chain's gas and nothing to oka.
