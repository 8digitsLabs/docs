## What you hold

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Am I buying a real share?">
    No. You are buying a wrapper: a token an issuer mints to track the stock's price. You get economic exposure, not legal title, not a vote, and no entry on any shareholder register.

    Your claim is on the issuer that minted the token. [What you own](/concepts/what-you-own) names the five of them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to dividends and splits?">
    They are handled by the issuer, and not all of them do it the same way, some scale the displayed balance, some apply a multiplier, some rebase the token in your wallet. The method for each issuer is listed in [chains and issuers](/reference/chains-and-issuers).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I trade at any hour?">
    Only when a listing is open. Wrappers track stock-market hours, and a venue that will not quote outside them takes its listing out of the running. Outside market hours you may find fewer listings, or none, available for a given stock.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Money and keys

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Who holds my keys?">
    You do. Signing in creates an EVM wallet and a Solana wallet, and either can be exported from the account menu. oka never reads them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Do I need ETH or SOL to pay gas?">
    No. oka pays the network's gas and charges you the equivalent in stables, so you never have to hold a native balance on five chains just to trade. That line is the [gas take](/reference/costs).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which stablecoin do I send?">
    USDC on every chain except Robinhood Chain, which has no USDC and settles in USDG, the Global Dollar. The deposit screen always names the correct token for the chain you picked, send anything else and it cannot be recovered.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can oka move my money without asking?">
    It can execute the steps of a trade you placed, and sweep a deposit to Base. That is what the session key covers. It cannot withdraw. Sending money to an address outside your wallet requires your signature every time.

    The session key is attached automatically at sign-in, without a prompt, and there is no button to revoke it. See [wallets and keys](/concepts/wallets-and-keys).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Orders

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My order failed. Where is my money?">
    As cash on Base. A buy is all-or-nothing: if the quote goes stale or the swap fails, no wrapper is bought. If your stables had already bridged to reach a listing, oka brings them home.

    One case does not resolve cleanly: a fill that lands while the trip home fails. The wrapper is yours on that chain, and oka names it in your activity rather than reporting success.

    Retrying reuses that cash, skips the bridge when the money is already in the right place, and re-runs the comparison, so it may pick a different listing than the one that just failed.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my trade more expensive on Ethereum?">
    Gas. It is charged per transaction by the chain, not by oka, and it has nothing to do with your order size, which means it falls hardest on small trades. That is also why Ethereum's minimums are higher: \$10 to trade against \$5 elsewhere, \$20 to deposit, \$25 to withdraw.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I trade right now?">
    One action runs at a time. A trade cannot start while a deposit is still bridging to Base, and a second trade cannot start while the first is in flight. Both take seconds, and the screen says which one is holding things up.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I place a limit order?">
    Not today, only market orders. Limit and TWAP orders are built and waiting to be switched on.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why did oka pick that issuer?">
    Because that listing left you with the most stock for your money at the moment you asked, bridge and spread included. The headline price is only one term in that sum, so the winner is often not the one with the best sticker.

    If you want to choose the issuer and chain yourself, use [Expert mode](/concepts/routing).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why can't I choose the exchange it trades on?">
    Because it is not a decision with a good answer. Which pool the swap crosses is a routing problem, solved by an aggregator against live liquidity. Neither Auto nor Expert exposes it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
