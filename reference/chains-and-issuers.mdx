Base is where oka operates: every balance is held there and every order is priced from there. It is the only chain with no deposit fee, because there is nothing to bridge.

Ethereum's numbers are higher for one reason, gas. A \$5 deposit that costs \$3 to send is not a deposit worth making.

<Note>
  **Robinhood Chain has no USDC.** It settles in USDG, the Global Dollar. The deposit screen names the right token for whichever chain you pick. Other tokens are not picked up yet; a token sent on a chain oka does not support may not be recoverable.
</Note>

Trade minimums apply **per listing**, not per order: a \$7 buy stops considering Ethereum rather than being refused.

Each issuer mints its own wrappers under its own terms. Two wrappers on the same stock are different instruments with different counterparties, which is why Expert mode lets you choose between them.

### How corporate actions are handled

A split or a dividend has to reach the token somehow, and the issuers do not agree on how.

- **Scaled UI**: the balance is scaled for display; the raw token amount does not change.
- **Multiplier**: a factor applied on top of the balance.
- **Rebase**: the token balance itself changes in your wallet.

The mechanism belongs to the issuer, not to oka. Which one you hold matters when a split lands.
