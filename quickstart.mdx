Three steps stand between a new account and a position. None of them asks you to install anything.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Email, Google, X or a passkey. A wallet is created for you in the same moment — one EVM smart account and one Solana keypair — so there is no extension to install and no seed phrase to write down.

    The keys are yours. You can export both from the Wallet screen whenever you want, and oka never reads them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Deposit stables">
    Send USDC to your deposit address, or connect a wallet you already use and transfer from it in one click.

    Solana is the default because it is the cheapest way in: **$1** minimum, and network fees around a cent. Ethereum works too, but its minimum is **$15** — not a rule oka invented, just what makes sense when a transfer costs a few dollars in gas.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy">
    Pick a stock, type an amount in dollars, confirm. oka compares every open listing for that stock, all-in, and fills the cheapest.

    The quote you confirm is the price you get. If it cannot be met, nothing is bought and your stables stay where they are.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What "all-in" means

The number oka minimizes is not the token price. It is everything you pay between the stables you hold now and the position you end up with: moving your money to the chain where the listing lives, the swap itself, the gas that the transactions burn, and oka's fee.

A listing with a better headline price and a thin book routinely loses to one that looks worse and costs less to reach. That comparison is the product.

## All-or-nothing

An order fills at the quoted all-in price or it does not happen. There is no partial fill that leaves you with a third of a position and no explanation.

If the quote goes stale or the swap fails, no wrapper is bought and your money stays in your execution wallet as **cash**, ready for the next attempt. A retry starts from scratch and reuses that cash first — no second transfer, and no second bridge if the stables are already on the right chain.

<Warning>
  Minimum trade sizes are per chain: **$1** on Solana, Base, Arbitrum and Robinhood, **$2** on BSC, **$10** on Ethereum. They exist so that gas never eats a meaningful share of what you are buying.
</Warning>

## After the fill

Your position shows up on the portfolio screen, inside the single dollar figure at the top. Selling is the same machinery inverted, and withdrawing is a plain transfer you sign yourself.
