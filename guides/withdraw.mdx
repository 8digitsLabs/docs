---
title: "Withdraw"
description: "Send stables out to any address you control. You sign it, and oka takes nothing."
---

Withdrawing sends stables from your execution wallet to an address you name. oka charges nothing for it — you pay the network's gas, and that is the whole cost.

## How it works

Choose a chain, an amount and a destination, then sign. The transfer is signed by **you**, every time. The session grant that lets oka execute your orders without a prompt does not extend to withdrawals, deliberately: the one action that can move money away from you is the one action that always asks.

Check the destination address and the chain before signing. A transfer to a wrong address is final.

## Minimums and fees

| Chain | Minimum | Network fee, roughly |
|---|---|---|
| Solana | $2 | $0.01 |
| Base | $2 | $0.02 |
| Robinhood | $2 | $0.02 |
| Arbitrum One | $2 | $0.04 |
| BSC | $2 | $0.08 |
| Ethereum | $25 | $3.40 |

Network fees are indicative and move with the chain. Ethereum's minimum is higher because its gas is; the rest are there so a withdrawal is never mostly fee.

<Note>
  Withdrawals send the chain's settlement stable — USDC everywhere, USDG on Robinhood Chain. To take money off a chain you are not on, sell or move first, then withdraw.
</Note>

## If you would rather leave with the keys

You can. Both halves of your execution wallet — the EVM smart account and the Solana keypair — can be exported from the Wallet screen, and oka never reads them. Exporting is not the same as withdrawing: it hands you control of the wallet itself, wrappers included.
