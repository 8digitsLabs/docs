---
title: "Deposit"
description: "Two ways to get stables into your execution wallet, and the one mistake that cannot be undone."
---

Everything on oka is bought with stablecoins held in your **execution wallet** — the wallet created for you at sign-in. Getting them there takes one of two forms.

## From a wallet you already use

Connect it once, pick an amount, sign. oka reads the balances it can spend and moves them same-chain into your execution wallet. This is the shortest path if you already hold stables somewhere, and the transfer is signed by you, not by oka.

## From an address

Open the deposit screen, choose a chain, and send to the address shown. A QR code is there for phones.

<Warning>
  Send only the token named on that screen, on the chain named on that screen. Stables sent on the wrong network, or a token that is not the one listed, cannot be recovered — not by oka and not by anyone.
</Warning>

## Which chain to use

Solana is the default because it is the cheapest way in. Ethereum works, but its minimum is higher for a reason that has nothing to do with oka: a transfer there can cost several dollars in gas, and depositing $5 to pay $3 of network fees is not a deposit worth making.

| Chain | Token accepted | Minimum |
|---|---|---|
| Solana | USDC | $1 |
| Base | USDC | $1 |
| Arbitrum One | USDC | $1 |
| BSC | USDC (Binance-Peg) | $1 |
| Robinhood | USDG | $1 |
| Ethereum | USDC | $15 |

Robinhood Chain is the one exception to "send USDC": it has no USDC, and settles in **USDG**, the Global Dollar. The deposit screen names it for you.

## After it lands

oka notices the deposit on its own — there is nothing to click. If the stables arrived somewhere other than where your next order needs them, the move happens as part of that order, not as a separate step you have to think about.

Deposited stables sit as **cash** until you buy something. Cash is reused before any new transfer, so money that came back from a failed order or a sale is spent first.
