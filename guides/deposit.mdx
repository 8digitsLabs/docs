You need stables before you can buy anything. There are two ways in, and both end in the same place.

## Two routes in

**From a wallet you already use.** Connect it, pick the chain, type an amount, sign one transfer. oka sees it arrive and takes it from there.

**To your deposit address.** Every chain has one. Copy it and send from anywhere — an exchange, a friend, another wallet — and it is picked up on the next check.

<Note>
  **Each chain settles in one stablecoin**, and the deposit screen names it before it shows you an address: USDC on five chains, **USDG on Robinhood Chain**, which has no USDC. Other tokens are not picked up yet. They stay in your execution wallet, whose keys you hold.
</Note>

<Warning>
  Send only on one of the six supported chains. A token sent on a chain oka does not support may not be recoverable.
</Warning>

## Minimums

**\$10** on Solana, Base, Arbitrum and BSC. **\$20** on Ethereum and Robinhood Chain.

A deposit below the line is not picked up. It stays where you sent it, and once you top it up past the minimum the whole balance is swept. The floor is there because the sweep has to cover its own gas and the \$1 fee.

## What happens next

Everything is consolidated into **USDC on Base**, where your balance lives. The sweep runs as soon as the deposit is detected, not when you next place an order.

It costs **\$1**, flat, taken out of what arrives, and it pays for the bridge. Deposit on Base and there is nothing to bridge, so there is no fee.

<Note>
  Above **\$10,000** a deposit can carry up to an extra **0.1%**, shown on the screen before you send.
</Note>

While the sweep runs your account is busy: a trade cannot start until the bridge lands. It takes seconds, and the dialog tracks it.

## When it does not arrive

A deposit that fails to bridge is not lost. The stables are still on the chain you sent them to, in a wallet you control, and oka retries on its own.

The detection check runs when you open the portfolio and while the deposit dialog is open. If you sent money and nothing appears, open the dialog.
