Pick a stock, type an amount in dollars, confirm. What sits between those is a comparison.

## The comparison

oka prices the stock on **every listing that carries it**, each issuer, on each chain, and ranks them by what you actually end up holding for your money.

That number is not the sticker price. It is the sticker price after the bridge from Base to the listing's chain, after the spread you cross in its pool, and after the gas the transactions burn.

Several venues may quote the same listing; oka keeps the best of them and compares listings. Quotes that come back implausibly good are dropped: a fill fourteen times better than the market is a bug.

## Auto and Expert

**Auto** picks for you, on cost, and does not show the alternatives it rejected.

**Expert** shows every listing side by side with its all-in cost and lets you choose the issuer and chain. Use it when you have a reason to hold one issuer's paper over another's: they are different instruments with different counterparties.

Neither mode lets you choose the execution venue. Which pool the swap crosses is left to the aggregator.

<Note>
  An Expert pick is honoured only if it can execute. A listing with no live quote is not selectable, and the ticket says so.
</Note>

## What you end up with

The wrapper lands in your execution wallet on the listing's chain, and the position appears on the portfolio screen inside the single dollar figure at the top.

A buy is all-or-nothing. If the quote goes stale, the swap reverts, or the route fails, nothing is bought and your money stays as cash on Base, ready for the next attempt. If the stables had already bridged to reach a listing that then failed, they are brought home.

<Warning>
  In rare cases a fill lands but the trip home does not. oka names it in your activity: the wrapper is yours, on that chain, and support can finish the move.
</Warning>
