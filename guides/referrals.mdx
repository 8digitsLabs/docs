## What you earn

**40% of the platform fee** on every trade they make, for as long as they trade. The fee is 0.25%, so a referred account trading \$10,000 pays \$25, of which \$10 is yours.

It comes out of oka's share: someone who arrives through your link pays exactly what they would have paid anyway.

Referrals also earn points. Points come from using the app, and they decide how the community incentive is split. The people you bring in count, and so does the stock volume they trade. Trading OKA or IOKA does not. The fee share above is separate, and it is paid in stables.
