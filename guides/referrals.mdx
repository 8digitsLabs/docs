## What you earn

**40% of the platform fee** on every trade they make, for as long as they trade. The fee is 0.25%. Someone who arrives through your link pays **10% less**, so a \$10,000 trade is a \$22.50 fee instead of \$25. 40% of that fee is yours: \$9.

The discount and your share both come out of oka's cut.
