## What you earn

**40% of the platform fee** on every trade they make, for as long as they trade. The fee is 0.25%, so a referred account trading \$10,000 pays \$25, of which \$10 is yours.

It comes out of oka's share, not theirs. Someone who arrives through your link pays exactly what they would have paid anyway.

## Points

Referrals also earn points, and points decide the airdrop. Both the people you bring in and the volume they trade count, on top of the fee share.
